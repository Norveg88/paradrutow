Frequently Asked Questions

What does each PDF pattern include?

Each pattern includes a chart with a symbol grid, a symbol legend, instructions on reading the chart, yarn and needle recommendations, and practical tips. The files are in Polish, and some patterns also have the legend in English — the language is shown on every card.

Who are these patterns for?

Patterns are marked with a difficulty level. The current lace patterns (Lace Leaf, Powder Leaf) are for intermediate knitters who know the basics of knitting and reading charts.

How do I get the file after purchase?

Right after purchase you will receive an email with a link to download the PDF file. The file is also available in your Payhip account.

Can I use a pattern commercially?

The patterns are for personal use only. Copying, reselling or commercial use of the charts is not permitted.

What yarn is recommended for lace patterns?

Lace patterns work best with DK or sport weight yarn in light, solid colours (cotton, linen, merino, silk). Blocking is essential — it fully opens up the lace.

Can I return a PDF pattern I bought?

PDF patterns purchased via Payhip are non-refundable once downloaded, as they are digital products. If a file won’t open or is damaged, write to us and we’ll help.
