Before you write

Answers to the most common questions — yours may be here too.

I bought a PDF pattern — where is the file?

The download link appears right after purchase and is also sent to the email address you used to pay. Can’t find it? Check your spam folder or write to us with that email address.

What language are the patterns and books in?

Pattern PDFs are in Polish; charts come with a symbol legend in Polish and English. Every book is marked with its language.

Where can I buy the books?

On Amazon and Payhip — you’ll find the store link next to each book.

Something isn’t working in a pattern or video.

Tell us which video or pattern it is (ideally with the minute in the video), which yarn and needles you use, and where the problem appears. The more details, the easier it is to help.

Can I suggest a video topic?

Of course! Tell us what you’d like to knit — we’re happy to read suggestions and take them into account when planning new videos.

Can I sell the pattern or share the PDF file?

No — the patterns are for personal use only and may not be copied or resold.
